Color tokens

A vibrant indigo accent on neutral cool grays — a deliberate revision from S3K's current shipped violet --accent in frontend/app/globals.css, requested for a more restrained, enterprise-professional read with enough richness to not feel flat (see REVIEW.md). One accent color used consistently, muted semantic colors reserved for status only — no decorative rainbow accents, no Salesforce blue.
